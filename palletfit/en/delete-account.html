PalletFit Account Deletion Request

Developer: somcat

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the PalletFit app.
  2. Go to Settings › Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email if you cannot use the app

Please send an email to the address below from the Google account email address you used to sign in.

To: somcat_0@naver.com
Subject: PalletFit account deletion request
Body: the Google account email you signed in with

After verifying your identity, we will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Data that is deleted

  • Account information: email, name, profile picture, account identifier
  • Cloud backup: SKUs, vehicles, pallets, box specifications, rules, orders
  • Trial record: the Pro trial start date recorded in your account
  • Subscription records: your customer record at RevenueCat (payment records are kept by Google Play)

Data that remains

  • Data stored on each device is not deleted. You can remove it by uninstalling the app or with Settings › Data › Reset.
  • Payment records for paid subscriptions are kept for 5 years as required by the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (Korea), and then deleted.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
