Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

somcat (솜캣) (the "Company") provides the PalletFit app (the "Service"). In accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of Korea and other applicable laws, the Company has established this Privacy Policy to protect users' personal information and to handle related concerns promptly and smoothly.

Summary
  • The SKUs, vehicles, pallets and orders you enter, and all calculations, are stored and processed on your device. If you do not sign in, nothing is stored on the Company's servers.
  • Even if you do not sign in, an anonymous purchaser ID and device and app information are sent to RevenueCat when the app starts to check your subscription status, and on the free tier your advertising ID and related data are sent to Google (AdMob) to show ads (see Section 1).
  • If you sign in with a Google account, we keep your email, name, profile picture and your cloud backup data.
  • On the free tier, your device's advertising ID may be shared with an advertising company (Google) to show ads.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your account and cloud backup immediately.

2. Retention period

  • Account and cloud backup: until you delete your account. When you request deletion, we delete them without delay.
  • Subscription records (RevenueCat): when you delete your account, your customer record at RevenueCat is deleted as well. Records of the payment itself are kept by Google Play.
  • Cloud backups that are no longer synced after switching to the free tier: may be deleted once 1 year has passed since the last sync. We will notify you by email 30 days before deletion.
  • Payment records: payments are processed by Google Play, and payment and subscription records are kept by Google Play and our processor RevenueCat. Records the Company keeps itself regarding contracts, cancellations, refunds and disputes (such as inquiry emails) are kept under the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (Korea): records of contracts and cancellations of purchases for 5 years, records of payment and supply of goods for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution for 3 years.
  • Inquiry records: 1 year after the inquiry is resolved.

3. Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except where required by law, such as a request from an investigative agency.

5. Deletion procedure and method

Personal information is deleted without delay once its retention period has expired or the purpose of processing has been achieved. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information.
  • In the app: go to Settings › Account › Delete account to delete your account and cloud backup immediately.
  • If you cannot use the app: you can make a request using the method on the Account Deletion Request page.
  • Changing ad consent: in the app, go to Settings › About › Ad privacy settings (in applicable regions), or delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Children under 14 may not sign up for the Service.

7. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS); per-account access control (row-level security: you can only read and write your own data)
  • Minimal administrative privileges; secret keys are never included in the app

8. Automatic collection tools

The app does not use web cookies. It stores an authentication token in device storage to keep you signed in. For the advertising ID, see Section 1. The app does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals; you can manage ad tracking with the ad privacy settings in Section 6 and the Android advertising ID settings.
